Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how yourlimitedcompany (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you contact us through this website.

Information we collect

If you email or call us, we may process the personal information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your enquiry.

How we use your information

We use contact details to:

  • Respond to your request

  • Improve our website experience where relevant

We do not sell your personal information.

Legal basis

Where UK GDPR applies, we process enquiry data based on our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, including pages viewed and clicks on key links such as email, WhatsApp and call-to-action buttons. Google may process technical data such as approximate location, device type and interaction events according to Google’s policies.

Sharing

We may use trusted service providers (for example email, hosting or analytics providers) that process information on our behalf only as needed to operate the website and communicate with you.

Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence only for as long as needed to respond and manage related follow-up, unless a longer period is required for legal or operational reasons.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing.
