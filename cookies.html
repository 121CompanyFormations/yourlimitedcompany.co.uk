Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how yourlimitedcompany uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function and can provide information about usage.

How we use cookies

This website uses essential cookies/local storage needed for basic operation, and may use analytics cookies from Google Analytics to understand traffic and link clicks (for example WhatsApp, email and CTA buttons).

Third-party services

Google Analytics and Google Fonts may process technical data (such as IP address, browser details and interaction events) according to their own policies when your browser connects to their servers.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.
